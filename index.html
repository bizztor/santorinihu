Our Story

Real Greek Flavours, Made With Heart

At Santorini Hut, every dish is a little island getaway. We marinate our meats in olive oil, lemon and oregano, grill our halloumi to a golden finish, and wrap it all in warm Greek pita with our world-famous tzatziki.

Inspired by the whitewashed streets and blue domes of Santorini, our kitchen is built on one simple belief — fresh ingredients, generous portions and the honest taste of home-style Greek cooking.

  • 🌿 Fresh, locally-sourced produce
  • 🔥 Char-grilled the traditional way
  • 🥙 Famous house-made tzatziki sauce
